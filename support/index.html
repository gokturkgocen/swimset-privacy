Help with SwimSet

SwimSet writes a pool session on iPhone and guides it on Apple Watch or a Garmin watch. If something is not working, this page covers the usual causes. For anything else, write to the address at the bottom.

iPhone: iOS 18 or later Apple Watch: watchOS 11 or later Garmin: Connect IQ 5.0 watches

Apple Watch

The session is not on my watch
Open SwimSet on the watch once, with the iPhone nearby and unlocked, then send again from Today. SwimSet shows on the iPhone whether the watch has the exact session you see, and says so when it is still waiting.
I also scheduled it in Apple's Workout app
Starting the same swim from both apps records two swims. Start it from SwimSet on the watch.

Garmin

Connecting a Garmin watch
Install the SwimSet app on your watch from the Connect IQ Store and keep Garmin Connect installed on the iPhone. In SwimSet, open Settings → Garmin → Connect a Garmin watch; Garmin Connect opens so you can choose the watch. After that, Today shows Send to Garmin.
Bluetooth permission
SwimSet asks for Bluetooth only when you connect a Garmin, because that is how the session reaches the watch. If you declined it, allow it again in the iPhone's Settings → SwimSet.

Health

My swims do not appear
SwimSet reads your swims from Apple Health. Check Health → Sharing → Apps → SwimSet and allow Workouts. Swims recorded on a Garmin reach Health only if Garmin Connect is set to share with Apple Health.

Subscription and purchases

Restore a purchase
Settings → Purchase → Restore purchases, on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.
Cancel or change a subscription
Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel them from Settings → Purchase in SwimSet, or in the iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

SwimSet has no account and no servers, and collects nothing. The details are in the privacy policy.
